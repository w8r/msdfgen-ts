msdfgen-ts — perf report

Live, in-browser companion to tools/bench.mjs and tools/bench-atlas-text.mjs — same methodology, run in this browser's JS engine instead of Node, so a viewer can see the real number for their own machine/browser rather than trusting a number from CI or a different machine. Not a gate: gate:m6 (Node, tools/bench.mjs) is the actual pass/fail authority — see docs/m6-perf-investigation.md for the full writeup this page's numbers are checked against.

Per-glyph generation (Roboto.ttf, 48px/em, pxrange 4)

Matches tools/bench.mjs's methodology exactly (fresh Atlas per iteration, so every generation is genuinely uncached), over a wide representative range — full Latin upper/lowercase, digits, common punctuation, and a Cyrillic sample, matching CLAUDE.md M0's corpus scope — rather than a handful of curated cases. Median of 15 runs after warmup (fewer than the Node tool's 200 — enough for a stable median across ~150 glyphs without freezing the tab). CLAUDE.md's M6 gate: median < 3ms.
